Vanishing Point Convergence Tester

Draw lines along parallel edges in an image — real photos converge at a single vanishing point; AI-generated images often don't.

Technique developed by Hany Farid (UC Berkeley) and collaborators in digital forensics. Based on research described in Science, 2025. Tool inspired by Farid's work on geometry-based deepfake detection.

How to use:
1. Upload an image.
2. Add a line group (each gets its own color).
3. Select a group, then draw lines along parallel edges (floor tiles, walls, object edges).
4. Extended lines show whether they converge — real images should meet at a single vanishing point.

Convergence score: lower = better convergence = more likely real.
